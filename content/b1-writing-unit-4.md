[writing]
text: "Write this essay in English using the Chinese structure guide"

answer: "Three years ago I began sorting my rubbish seriously, and at first it felt like a bother I could do without. || Over time, however, I realised that every sheet of paper and every bottle has a proper destination instead of a landfill. Recycling a single tonne of paper can save more than a dozen trees, which is a fact that still surprises me whenever I think about it. Now, when I see clean streets, I feel a quiet sense of relief. || Protecting the environment is not one grand act; it is the small choices we repeat day after day until they become who we are."
chineseEssay: "三年前，我开始做垃圾分类。一开始觉得很麻烦，但后来我明白每一张纸、每一个瓶子都有它的去处。回收一吨纸就能拯救许多棵树。现在看到干净的街道，我心里就有一种踏实感。环保不是一次惊天动地的大事，而是每天做出的小小选择。"
core: "Three years ago I started sorting rubbish. At first it seemed troublesome, but I learned every paper and bottle has a place. Recycling a tonne of paper saves many trees. Now clean streets give me a calm feeling. Environmental care is small daily choices, not one big act."
acceptAlso: "I took up recycling in earnest three years back, and though it struck me as tiresome early on, I grew to see that each scrap and bottle belongs somewhere useful. || A tonne of reused paper spares over ten trees, a number that moved me to continue. Today tidy streets bring a private satisfaction I did not expect from such a small habit. || Green living is no giant gesture but the humble, repeated decisions we make at home, and they quietly add up."
srsWords: "recycle" "rubbish" "paper" "trees" "choice"

I eat less meat and cycle more. The solution is turning our anxiety into concrete daily action that we repeat consistently."
acceptAlso: "To me, global warming is the pressing challenge of our time, already reshaping the world we know and the one we will leave. || Severe storms and heat arrive more often, hitting vulnerable shore towns hardest while the rest watch. I now eat fewer animal products and ride a bike whenever I can. || The answer lies in converting worry into the small deeds we perform routinely, not in waiting for others."
