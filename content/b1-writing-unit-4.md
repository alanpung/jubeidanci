description: "Write about why you recycle"
icon: "🌍"
color: "#059669"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "三年前我开始认真垃圾分类。起初觉得麻烦，但慢慢发现，每一张纸、每个瓶子都有去处。回收一吨纸能救十几棵树。现在看到干净的街道，我有种踏实感。环保不是大事，而是日复一日的小选择。（请写成三段：开头点题、中间展开、结尾收束。字数100-150）"
answer: "Three years ago I began sorting my rubbish seriously, and at first it felt like a bother I could do without. || Over time, however, I realised that every sheet of paper and every bottle has a proper destination instead of a landfill. Recycling a single tonne of paper can save more than a dozen trees, which is a fact that still surprises me whenever I think about it. Now, when I see clean streets, I feel a quiet sense of relief. || Protecting the environment is not one grand act; it is the small choices we repeat day after day until they become who we are."
core: "Three years ago I started sorting rubbish. At first it seemed troublesome, but I learned every paper and bottle has a place. Recycling a tonne of paper saves many trees. Now clean streets give me a calm feeling. Environmental care is small daily choices, not one big act."
acceptAlso: "I took up recycling in earnest three years back, and though it struck me as tiresome early on, I grew to see that each scrap and bottle belongs somewhere useful. || A tonne of reused paper spares over ten trees, a number that moved me to continue. Today tidy streets bring a private satisfaction I did not expect from such a small habit. || Green living is no giant gesture but the humble, repeated decisions we make at home, and they quietly add up."
srsWords: "recycle" "rubbish" "paper" "trees" "choice"

[writing]

sentence: "我认为气候变化是这代人最紧迫的课题。极端天气越来越多，海边的小镇首当其冲。个人力量虽小，但亿万人加起来就不同了。我选择少吃肉、多骑车。解决问题的钥匙，在于把焦虑变成每天的行动。（请写成三段：开头点题、中间展开、结尾收束。字数100-150）"

I have chosen to eat less meat and cycle more often than before. || In my view the key to the problem is turning our anxiety into concrete action taken every single day, not once a year."
core: "I think climate change is the most urgent issue for our generation. Extreme weather is more common, and coastal towns suffer first. One person's effort is small, but millions together matter. I eat less meat and cycle more. The solution is turning our anxiety into concrete daily action that we repeat consistently."
acceptAlso: "To me, global warming is the pressing challenge of our time, already reshaping the world we know and the one we will leave. || Severe storms and heat arrive more often, hitting vulnerable shore towns hardest while the rest watch. Alone our impact seems slight, but billions acting together shift the curve downward. I now eat fewer animal products and ride a bike whenever I can. || The answer lies in converting worry into the small deeds we perform routinely, not in waiting for others."
srsWords: "climate" "change" "weather" "coast" "action"

core: "In my city the metro is convenient, so I avoid driving. Cars mean traffic and pollution, but the subway is on time, cheap and lets me read. Late at night or in suburbs a car is still needed. I think future cities should give more room to buses and bikes."
acceptAlso: "My hometown's underground network is reliable enough that I seldom take a car anywhere nearby. || A vehicle offers liberty, true, but also congestion and smog, whereas trains run on time, cost little and let me read en route. Naturally, after midnight or out in the countryside a car remains useful for the isolated. || Yet I hold that tomorrow's cities ought to prioritise transit and cycling over parked, unused automobiles that eat our public space."

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我家附近有条小河，小时候清澈见底，后来被垃圾填了一半。去年我和邻居自发去清理，种了些柳树。如今河水又清了，还有小鸟回来。守护自然不一定要去远方，从门前开始就够。（请写成三段：开头点题、中间展开、结尾收束。字数100-150）"

core: "Near my home is a small river, clear when I was young, later half-filled with rubbish. Last year neighbours and I cleaned it and planted willows. Now the water is clear again and birds returned. Protecting nature need not mean going far; starting at your door works."
acceptAlso: "A stream by my house shone with fish in my childhood, then waste choked much of it over the years until it smelled. || Last spring my neighbours and I took matters into our hands, clearing the litter and setting young willows along the edge. The channel runs clean once more, and birds drift back to nest above it. || Caring for the earth need not happen far away; the work at our own gateway counts more than any distant good intention we never act on."
srsWords: "river" "rubbish" "neighbour" "willow" "bird"

acceptAlso: "Animals, to my mind, are far more than usable material for our convenience and comfort. || Lab subjects often bear quiet suffering we never see, and intensive farms raise real ethical doubts I cannot ignore. I take no radical line, yet I skip fur and back kinder agriculture with my wallet and my choices. || Honouring life need not mean humanity withdraws; it means we learn to coexist softly with the creatures around us, not above them as masters."

core: "To me green living is not a slogan but a daily practice: a carried cup, an off light, a walk. It reconnects me with nature and shows that my wants can be far fewer than I thought. Real wealth is having enough and leaving a clean world for the people of tomorrow."
acceptAlso: "Green living, in my book, is no trendy catchphrase but a grounded routine I return to without thinking. || It is the reusable bottle, the lamp switched dark, the trip made on foot when the weather and time permit. It ties me back to nature and whispers that desire can shrink without loss of joy. || Genuine richness, I now believe, lies in sufficiency and handing a pristine earth to the next generation, not in endless accumulation that costs the planet."
srsWords: "green" "living" "daily" "nature" "enough"
