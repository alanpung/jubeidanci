[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "Write about a memorable journey in English. || Key vocabulary hints: spring, train, village, market, tea"
answer: "Last spring my friend and I took a slow train to a neighbouring province, and the journey itself became the best part of the trip. || Through the window we watched terraced fields and golden rapeseed flowers stretch all the way to the horizon. At a tiny station we got off by chance and wandered into a village holding its weekly market. An old man there invited us to drink tea brewed by his own family. This unplanned journey, with no fixed schedule at all, turned out to be one of the most unforgettable memories I have ever made. || We ended up chatting with the villagers for nearly an hour before the next train came, and I realised the best parts of any trip are rarely the famous sights."
core: "Last spring my friend and I took a slow train to a neighbouring province. We saw terraced fields and golden flowers from the window. At a small station we got off and found a village market. An old man invited us to drink his homemade tea. This unplanned trip became my most unforgettable memory."
acceptAlso: "A spring trip with a friend on a local train took us to another province without a plan. || We admired yellow flower fields and steep terraces beyond the glass. We left the train at a minor stop and discovered a farming village at market time. A senior resident offered us family-made tea with a smile. The journey with no itinerary became my favourite recollection, proving the best moments are often unplanned. || Such simple encounters stay with me far longer than any postcard view ever could."
srsWords: "spring" "train" "village" "market" "tea"
