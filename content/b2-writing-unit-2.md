There is also a social dimension: new employees may struggle to learn office culture when they never meet colleagues informally. || To manage this, companies can organise regular team days and pair newcomers with experienced mentors. Another concern is self-discipline, since some workers find it hard to start and stop without a fixed schedule. Clear deadlines and outcomes-based assessment solve this problem more effectively than monitoring software, which damages trust. || On balance, flexibility should be offered wherever possible, with the option of office space for those who need it."
