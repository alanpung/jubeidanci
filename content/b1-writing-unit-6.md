lessonTitle: "The Importance of Sleep"
description: "Write about the importance of sleep"
icon: "🥗"
color: "#EA580C"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English in three short paragraphs: an opening, a middle and an ending"
sentence: "我以前总熬夜，白天昏昏沉沉。后来我尽量十一点前睡觉，发现差别很大：记东西更快，心情也稳定了。睡眠不是浪费时间，而是身体在悄悄修复自己。现在我把它当成每天最重要的事之一，再忙也不轻易牺牲。（请写成三段：开头点题、中间展开、结尾收束。）"
answer: "I used to stay up late almost every night, and the next day I would drag myself through the hours in a permanent fog, unable to concentrate or remember things clearly. || Eventually I made a real effort to be in bed before eleven, and the difference surprised me. My memory improved, my mood became far more even, and I had enough energy to enjoy the evening instead of merely surviving it. Sleep is not wasted time in any sense; it is the quiet period when our bodies repair themselves and our brains organise everything we have learned during the day. || These days I treat a good night's rest as one of the most important appointments I have, and no matter how busy my schedule becomes, I try hard not to sacrifice it."
core: "I once stayed up late and felt tired every day. Sleeping before eleven changed everything: better memory, steadier mood and more energy. Sleep is not wasted time but when our bodies recover. Now I protect my sleep even when busy. I now keep screens away from the bedroom at night."
acceptAlso: "Late nights once left me exhausted and unfocused, but going to bed earlier transformed my days. || My thinking sharpened, my emotions settled and my energy returned. Rest is not idleness; it is essential repair for body and mind. I now guard my sleep carefully, knowing that everything else works better when I am properly rested. || I also keep my phone away from the bed and read a few pages instead, so falling asleep has become much easier and my mornings feel brighter."
srsWords: "sleep" "late" "energy" "memory" "rest" "routine"

text: "Write this passage in English in three short paragraphs: an opening, a middle and an ending"
sentence: "我最喜欢的运动是慢跑。每天傍晚，我会绕着公园跑半小时。刚开始跑几百米就喘得厉害，现在能轻松跑完几公里。跑步不仅让我身体更强壮，也让我把一天的烦恼都甩在身后。跑到终点时，那种痛快的感觉让我特别满足。（请写成三段：开头点题、中间展开、结尾收束。）"
answer: "My favourite form of exercise is jogging, and I usually run around the park for about half an hour every evening after the heat of the day has passed. || When I first started, even a few hundred metres left me breathless and desperate to stop, but little by little my stamina grew and now I can cover several kilometres without any real difficulty. Running does far more than strengthen my body; it also gives me time alone with my thoughts, and I feel as though I leave the day's small worries behind me with every step. By the time I reach the end of my route, a wonderful sense of freedom and satisfaction washes over me. || It has become the part of the day I look forward to most, because it leaves me both physically stronger and mentally lighter."
core: "My favourite exercise is jogging in the park for half an hour each evening. At first I could barely run a few hundred metres, but now I manage several kilometres. Running makes me fitter and clears away daily worries. The happy feeling at the end keeps me coming back."
acceptAlso: "Jogging around the park each evening is the exercise I love most. || I began unable to run a short distance, yet steady practice built my endurance until several kilometres felt easy. It strengthens my body and, just as importantly, clears the day's stress from my mind. That rush of joy at the finish line is why I never want to stop. || I usually run with a friend at weekends, and the chat makes the kilometres pass almost unnoticed."

---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English in three short paragraphs: an opening, a middle and an ending"
sentence: "我以前爱喝甜饮料、吃油炸食品，结果常常觉得没精神。半年前我开始改变：多喝水，多吃蔬菜和水果，少吃零食。坚持下来后，我的皮肤变好了，体重也正常了。健康饮食不是苦行，而是让自己感觉更好的一种选择。（请写成三段：开头点题、中间展开、结尾收束。）"
answer: "I used to love sweet drinks and fried food, and as a result I often felt tired and heavy for no obvious reason. || About six months ago I decided to make a genuine change. I began drinking more water, filling my plate with vegetables and fruit, and cutting down sharply on snacks between meals. It was not easy at first, because old habits pull strongly, but I kept at it and slowly the results became clear. My skin looks healthier, my weight has settled at a more normal level, and I rarely feel that sluggish afternoon tiredness any more. To me, eating healthily is not a form of punishment or a strict diet that forbids everything enjoyable. || It is simply a thoughtful choice that helps me feel better in my own body, and that is a reward worth keeping."
core: "I once ate lots of junk food and felt tired. Six months ago I switched to water, vegetables and fruit with fewer snacks. My skin and weight improved, and my energy returned. Healthy eating is not punishment but a choice to feel better. Small treats keep the habit sustainable."
acceptAlso: "Sweet drinks and fried snacks once left me sluggish, so half a year ago I changed my habits. || I drink more water, eat plenty of produce and avoid constant snacking. Though difficult initially, the effort paid off with clearer skin, steadier weight and better energy. Healthy eating is a positive choice, not a joyless restriction. || I still enjoy a small treat now and then, because a healthy diet must be sustainable rather than painfully strict and boring."
srsWords: "diet" "vegetables" "water" "snacks" "energy" "habit"

Then I step outside for a short walk, because changing the environment helps me see my problems from a fresh angle. Finally I write down everything I need to do and tackle the tasks one at a time, which turns a scary mountain into a manageable list. Stress does not vanish completely, of course, but it no longer crushes me. || The most important thing is not to bottle it up, and to find an outlet that genuinely suits my own character."
core: "Under stress my chest feels tight. I cope by breathing deeply, taking a short walk outside, and listing tasks to do one by one. Stress does not disappear, but these steps stop it overwhelming me. The key is not to hide my feelings and to find a healthy outlet."
acceptAlso: "Pressure once left me tense and unable to think, but I have developed a routine that helps. || Slow breathing settles my body, a brief walk shifts my perspective, and a simple to-do list turns chaos into order. The strain remains, yet I can carry it. Keeping feelings inside only makes them heavier, so finding a personal outlet truly matters. || Talking to a friend often helps as much as the breathing, because sharing a problem makes it feel smaller."
srsWords: "stress" "breathe" "walk" "tasks" "outlet" "calm"

Talking honestly with someone I trust is as important to me as any run. || Both needs deserve attention every single day, not only when something has gone wrong."

This habit has taught me that looking after your body does not require expensive equipment or hours at a gym. || What truly matters is doing a little something every single day, because small efforts repeated consistently add up to real, lasting change over time."

The lesson is clear: good health needs no grand effort, just a small kind act repeated every day. || I do the same stretches before bed now, and the routine has become something I genuinely miss when I travel."
