description: "Write about the importance of sleep"
icon: "🥗"
color: "#EA580C"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this essay in English using the Chinese structure guide"
sentence: "Write about the importance of sleep in English. || Requirements: 3-4 paragraphs, 100-150 words || Key vocabulary hints: sleep, late, energy, memory, rest, routine"
answer: "I used to stay up late almost every night, and the next day I would drag myself through the hours in a permanent fog, unable to concentrate or remember things clearly. || Eventually I made a real effort to be in bed before eleven, and the difference surprised me. My memory improved, my mood became far more even, and I had enough energy to enjoy the evening instead of merely surviving it. Sleep is not wasted time in any sense; it is the quiet period when our bodies repair themselves and our brains organise everything we have learned during the day. || These days I treat a good night's rest as one of the most important appointments I have, and no matter how busy my schedule becomes, I try hard not to sacrifice it."
chineseEssay: "我曾经熬夜，每天都觉得疲惫不堪。十一点前入睡改变了一切：记忆力更好了，情绪更稳定了，精力也更充沛了。睡眠不是浪费时间，而是身体恢复的时刻。现在即使再忙，我也会保护好我的睡眠。晚上我还把所有电子屏幕都搬出了卧室。"
core: "I once stayed up late and felt tired every day. Sleeping before eleven changed everything: better memory, steadier mood and more energy. Sleep is not wasted time but when our bodies recover. Now I protect my sleep even when busy. I now keep screens away from the bedroom at night."
acceptAlso: "Late nights once left me exhausted and unfocused, but going to bed earlier transformed my days. || My thinking sharpened, my emotions settled and my energy returned. Rest is not idleness; it is essential repair for body and mind. I now guard my sleep carefully, knowing that everything else works better when I am properly rested. || I also keep my phone away from the bed and read a few pages instead, so falling asleep has become much easier and my mornings feel brighter."
srsWords: "sleep" "late" "energy" "memory" "rest" "routine"

My skin looks healthier, my weight has settled at a more normal level, and I rarely feel that sluggish afternoon tiredness any more. core: "I once ate lots of junk food and felt tired. Six months ago I switched to water, vegetables and fruit with fewer snacks. My skin and weight improved, and my energy returned. Healthy eating is not punishment but a choice to feel better. Small treats keep the habit sustainable."
acceptAlso: "Sweet drinks and fried snacks once left me sluggish, so half a year ago I changed my habits. || I drink more water, eat plenty of produce and avoid constant snacking. Though difficult initially, the effort paid off with clearer skin, steadier weight and better energy. Healthy eating is a positive choice, not a joyless restriction. || I still enjoy a small treat now and then, because a healthy diet must be sustainable rather than painfully strict and boring."
srsWords: "diet" "vegetables" "water" "snacks" "energy" "habit"
