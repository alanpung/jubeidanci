[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我有一个最好的朋友，叫小丽。她有一双大眼睛，笑起来很好看。我们住在同一个小区。每天一起上学，一起回家。她画画很好，我写字很好。我们互相帮助。没有她，我会很孤单。我希望我们永远是朋友。"
answer: "I have a best friend named Xiao Li. She has big eyes and a pretty smile. We live in the same neighborhood. We go to school and come home together every day. She draws well and I write well. We help each other. Without her I would be lonely. I hope we are friends forever."
acceptAlso: "My best friend is Xiao Li. She has big eyes and smiles beautifully. We are neighbors. We walk to school and back daily. She is good at drawing; I am good at writing. We help one another. I would be lonely without her. May we stay friends."
