core: "Exams seem fair because all candidates face identical questions, limiting favouritism and giving clear evidence of achievement for university selection. Yet they encourage teaching to the test, memorisation and neglect of creativity. Alternatives such as portfolios may be costly and subjective. Therefore a mixed system works best, combining exams for core knowledge with continuous assessment of analysis, collaboration and original work. Clear criteria keep such assessment credible.
