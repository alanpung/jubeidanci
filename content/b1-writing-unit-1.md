lessonTitle: "A Childhood Memory"
description: "Write about a childhood memory"
icon: "🧭"
color: "#2563EB"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我小时候最喜欢去乡下的外婆家。夏天我们常去河边摘果子、捉小鱼。外婆耐心地教我读书，晚上还讲古老的故事。那些安静的下午让我感到安全又幸福。现在我长大了，但这些温暖的回忆一直留在我心里。"
answer: "When I was a child, my favourite place in the whole world was my grandmother's small house in the countryside. Every summer holiday I went there, and we often walked slowly down to the river, where we picked ripe fruit and tried to catch small fish with our hands. My grandmother was wonderfully patient with me; she taught me how to read my first books, and in the cool evenings she told me old family stories that I never grew tired of hearing. Those quiet, sunny afternoons made me feel completely safe and truly happy. Now that I am older and live in a busy city, I still look back on those summers with great warmth, and they remain among the happiest memories of my life."
core: "When I was a child, my favourite place was my grandmother's house in the countryside. Every summer we went to the river to pick fruit and catch small fish. My grandmother taught me to read and told me old stories in the evening. Those quiet afternoons made me feel safe and happy. Now I am older, but those warm memories stay in my heart."
acceptAlso: "As a young child I loved visiting my grandmother in her village. During the summer we used to walk to the river, where we gathered fruit and attempted to catch tiny fish together. Grandmother helped me learn to read with endless patience, and at night she shared traditional tales that fascinated me. I always felt calm and cheerful in those peaceful afternoons by her side. Although I have long since grown up and moved away, the gentle memories of those days still warm my heart whenever I think of them, and they remind me of a simpler, happier time I will always treasure."
srsWords: "childhood" "grandmother" "countryside" "river" "memory"

After three weeks of patience I could finally ride steadily along the street on my own, without anyone holding the seat. This experience taught me that with persistence even the most difficult things can be mastered in the end. To this day I still remember the wonderful feeling of freedom that riding gave me as a child."
core: "Learning to ride a bicycle was one of the hardest challenges of my childhood. At first I kept falling and my knees were bruised. But I did not give up and practised for half an hour every day after school. After three weeks I could finally ride steadily. This taught me that with persistence even difficult things can be learned. I still remember the happy feeling of freedom."
acceptAlso: "One of my toughest childhood struggles was learning to cycle. I fell off constantly at the beginning and hurt my legs badly, yet I was determined not to quit. I trained for thirty minutes daily once lessons finished, and slowly my balance improved. Within three weeks I was confidently cycling around the neighbourhood without help. The lesson stayed with me: keep trying and you can achieve almost anything. Even now that memory makes me smile with pride whenever I face something new."

Although it was embarrassing at the time, looking back now I find it a rather funny story to tell my friends. Since that evening I have paid even more attention to learning foreign expressions accurately, because one wrong word can change everything completely."
core: "Last week in a restaurant I wanted to order hot water, but by mistake I asked for foot-washing water and everyone laughed. My face turned red and I did not know what to do. The waiter was kind and quickly corrected my order. Although it was embarrassing, now I think it is a funny story. Since then I pay more attention to learning expressions correctly."
acceptAlso: "A few days ago I went to a cafe and meant to request a glass of warm water. Instead I confused the words and said something that meant washing feet, which made the whole room laugh. I blushed deeply and froze for a second, unsure how to recover. Luckily the staff member smiled and fixed the order politely, sparing my pride. It felt awkward then, yet today it is an amusing anecdote. I now study phrases more carefully so that I avoid such slips in future conversations."

Conquering that fear has made me far more confident in myself than I ever expected to be."

Overcoming that fear made me more confident."

This festival taught me that being together does not always require sharing the same roof; closeness lives in the heart."
