[writing]
text: "Translate this Chinese passage into English"
sentence: "Write about my favorite food in English. || Requirements: 1 paragraph, 50-80 words"
answer: "My favourite food is dumplings because they taste wonderful and feel like home to me. During festivals my mother makes them at home with her own hands for the family. core: "My favourite food is dumplings. On every festival, mother makes dumplings. Dumplings have meat and vegetables inside and smell good. I also help to shape them. Eating dumplings makes me feel the warmth of home and think of reunion."
acceptAlso: "My favourite food is dumplings. At festivals mum makes them by hand for us. They have meat and vegetables and smell nice when cooked. I help fold them into shape. Eating dumplings feels like home and reminds me of family reunions. It is the meal I look forward to most in the year. A warm meal feels like a small gift. I try to eat more vegetables each day. Cooking with family is fun for us. Water and fruit keep me fresh and light."
