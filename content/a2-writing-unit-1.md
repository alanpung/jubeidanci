[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我每天早上六点半起床。我先刷牙洗脸，然后吃早饭。七点半我坐公交车去学校。路上大约需要二十分钟。到了学校，我和朋友聊天。我喜欢早晨，因为一天开始了。"
answer: "I get up at half past six every morning. First I brush my teeth and wash my face, and then I have breakfast. At half past seven I take the bus to school. The trip takes about twenty minutes. When I arrive at school, I chat with my friends. I like the morning because a new day begins."
