answer: "Listening is often confused with simply waiting for an opportunity to speak, but genuine listening requires far more attention. || Active listening means focusing completely on the speaker and trying to understand both the words and the underlying emotions. However, most people interrupt mentally long before the other person has finished. Consequently, they respond to assumptions rather than to what was actually said. Moreover, offering immediate advice can make the speaker feel dismissed or misunderstood. On the other hand, silence without any response may seem cold and uninterested. || A balanced approach involves asking clarifying questions and briefly summarising what has been heard. Furthermore, non-verbal signals such as eye contact and nodding demonstrate that attention is genuine. It is important to resist the temptation to judge or prepare a defence while listening. When people feel truly heard, they become more willing to share honestly. Paraphrasing the key points occasionally confirms that both people understand each other, and this simple habit prevents many unnecessary arguments. || Ultimately, active listening transforms ordinary conversations into opportunities for trust and deeper understanding."
modelAnswerZh: "倾听常常与等待说话的机会混为一谈，但真正的倾听需要多得多的专注。 || 积极倾听意味着完全专注于说话者，努力理解话语及其背后的情感。然而，大多数人在对方还没说完之前很久，就已经在心里打断了。结果，他们回应的是自己的假设，而不是对方实际说的内容。此外，立刻给出建议会让说话者感到被忽视或被误解。另一方面，完全沉默、毫无回应又可能显得冷漠、不感兴趣。 || 平衡的做法是提出澄清性问题，并简要复述所听到的内容。此外，眼神交流和点头等非语言信号表明注意力是真诚的。倾听时，要抵制评判或准备辩解的诱惑，这很重要。当人们感到真正被听见时，他们会更愿意坦诚分享。偶尔释义要点能确认双方互相理解，而这个简单的习惯能避免许多不必要的争吵。 || 归根结底，积极倾听把普通对话变成了建立信任和加深理解的机会。"
core: "Active listening means concentrating on the speaker and understanding both words and emotions, not merely waiting to talk. Most people mentally interrupt and respond to assumptions, while instant advice can feel dismissive. Yet silent indifference is equally unhelpful. The solution is to ask clarifying questions, summarise key points, and use eye contact and nodding to show attention. Listeners should avoid judging or preparing replies. When individuals feel heard, they share more openly. Ultimately, active listening builds trust and deeper understanding."

Some disagreements reflect incompatible values, so respectful acceptance may be better than false agreement. Useful strategies include describing feelings without blame, pausing when emotions rise, and addressing the present issue only. Choosing the right time to talk also prevents unnecessary escalation.
