lessonTitle: "Lesson 1: My Body"
description: "Write an English essay about the body"
icon: "💪"
color: "#EF4444"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this essay in English"
sentence: "我的身体很健康。我有两只眼睛、两只耳朵和一只鼻子。我的嘴巴能说话，也能吃饭。我有两只手，可以用手写字和画画。我有两条腿和两只脚，可以走路和跑步。每天我都做运动，让身体更强壮。我很喜欢我的身体。"
answer: "I have a healthy body. I have two eyes, two ears, and one nose. My mouth can talk and eat. I have two hands, and I can write and draw with them. I have two legs and two feet, so I can walk and run. Every day I do exercise to make my body strong. I like my body very much."
acceptAlso: "My body is healthy. I have two eyes, two ears, and a nose. My mouth talks and eats. I have two hands to write and draw. I have two legs and two feet to walk and run. I exercise every day to stay strong. I love my body."
srsWords: "body" "eyes" "hands" "exercise" "strong"

[writing]
text: "Write this essay in English"
sentence: "手每天碰很多东西，所以要常洗。吃饭前我洗手。上完厕所我也洗手。我用肥皂，搓二十秒。手指缝和指甲都要洗到。然后用干净的毛巾擦干。干净的手不容易生病。妈妈教我认真洗手。我要坚持下去。"

acceptAlso: "Our hands touch a lot, so wash often. I wash before eating. I wash after the toilet too. I use soap and scrub twenty seconds. I clean the gaps and nails. Then I dry with a clean towel. Clean hands stay healthy. Mom taught me. I will keep it up."
