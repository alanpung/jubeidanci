lessonTitle: "My Best Friend"
description: "Write about your best friend"
icon: "🤝"
color: "#0D9488"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我最好的朋友叫小林。我们从小一起长大，彼此知道对方的习惯和小毛病。她最大的特点是善良又诚实，我难过时她会陪着我，我做错事时也会直说。我们不一定天天联系，但每次见面都像昨天才分开。真正的朋友，就是让你做自己的人。"
answer: "My best friend is Xiao Lin, and we have known each other since we were small children growing up in the same neighbourhood, so we understand each other's habits and little weaknesses better than anyone else could. What I value most about her is her rare combination of kindness and honesty. When I am low, she stays patiently by my side without needing me to explain, and when I behave foolishly she is brave enough to tell me the truth, which I have learned to appreciate even when it is uncomfortable to hear. We do not necessarily message each other every single day, and weeks can pass without contact, yet whenever we meet it feels as though we only saw one another yesterday. To me that is the mark of a real friend: someone in whose company I feel completely free to be myself, without pretending or performing."
core: "My best friend Xiao Lin and I grew up together, so we know each other well. She is kind and honest, comforting me when I am sad and telling me the truth when I am wrong. We may not talk daily, but we are always close. A real friend lets me be myself."
acceptAlso: "Xiao Lin has been my closest friend since childhood, and shared years have taught us each other's quirks. Her kindness comforts me in hard times while her honesty keeps me grounded. Though we may go weeks without talking, every reunion feels effortless. True friendship, I believe, means being totally accepted as I am. We also share our failures and silly mistakes, and laughing about them together makes them much easier to bear."
srsWords: "best" "friend" "childhood" "kind" "honest" "oneself" "close"

The meals she cooks, the gentle advice she offers and the patience she shows toward everyone around her are all filled with a warmth that is hard to describe. She has shown me that greatness does not have to be loud or dramatic; it can hide inside the most ordinary days, in a life of small, consistent kindness."
core: "I admire my grandmother, who had little schooling but taught me strength and goodness by example. She never complains and always puts others first. Her cooking, words and patience are full of warmth. She showed me greatness can live in ordinary kindness. I try to copy her patience every day."
acceptAlso: "My grandmother is the person I look up to most. Though poorly educated, she has lived a life of quiet strength and selflessness, rarely complaining and always considering others first. Her food, advice and endless patience radiate warmth. From her I learned that true greatness often hides in simple, everyday kindness. I try to copy her patience when I am tired or annoyed, and I am slowly learning how much a calm word can change."

icon: "🤝"
color: "#0D9488"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "有一次我在大雨里没带伞，站在路边发愁。一位陌生的阿姨把她的伞塞给我，自己冲进雨里。我想还给她，她只是笑着摆摆手。那把伞我一直留着。这件事让我相信，世界上还是有很多善良的人，也提醒我以后也要这样帮助别人。"
answer: "One afternoon I was caught in a sudden heavy rain without an umbrella, and I stood at the roadside feeling utterly helpless as the water poured down. Just as I was wondering what to do, a stranger, a middle-aged woman I had never met before, pressed her own umbrella into my hands and then ran off into the rain without a word. I called after her, trying to return it, but she only waved and disappeared around the corner, leaving me holding the warm handle and staring in surprise. I have kept that umbrella ever since, and I still think of her whenever the sky turns dark. That single act of generosity restored my faith in people, because it was given freely, with no expectation of anything in return. It also reminds me to be that kind of stranger for someone else whenever the chance arises."
core: "Once I was stuck in heavy rain without an umbrella. A stranger gave me hers and ran off, refusing to take it back. I still keep that umbrella. Her kindness restored my faith in people and inspires me to help others selflessly. I now carry an extra umbrella for others."
acceptAlso: "Caught in a downpour with no umbrella, I felt stranded until a kind stranger handed me hers and vanished into the rain, waving away my thanks. I have kept it ever since as a reminder of her generosity. That moment renewed my belief in human goodness and pushes me to help others without expecting reward. Whenever it rains now I carry an extra umbrella, hoping I might be that small kindness for someone else one day."
srsWords: "stranger" "rain" "umbrella" "kindness" "generosity" "faith"

lessonTitle: "Learning to Listen"
description: "Write about the importance of listening"
icon: "🤝"
color: "#0D9488"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我以前总急着说话，别人还没讲完我就想反驳。后来我发现，这样常常误会别人。现在我努力先听完，弄清楚对方真正的意思，再表达自己的看法。倾听不是沉默，而是尊重。它让我的关系变好了，也让我学到很多以前听不到的东西。"
answer: "I used to be in such a hurry to speak that I would start forming my reply before the other person had even finished, and more than once I realised too late that I had completely misunderstood them. That habit quietly damaged my relationships until I decided to change it. Now I make a real effort to hear someone out and to understand what they are truly trying to say before I offer my own opinion, even when I strongly disagree. I have discovered that listening is not the same as staying silent; it is an active, generous act that shows respect for the person in front of you. Since I began to listen properly, my friendships have grown warmer and more honest, and I have learned countless things that I would never have heard if I had kept filling the air with my own voice."
core: "I once interrupted people and misunderstood them often. Now I listen fully before replying, even when I disagree. Listening is not silence but respect. It improved my relationships and taught me things I had missed. People open up about their real worries only when they feel truly heard."
acceptAlso: "Formerly I cut in before others finished and frequently got their meaning wrong. I have since learned to hear people out and grasp their real point before responding. Listening is an active sign of respect, not mere quietness. It has deepened my friendships and opened my mind to ideas I once talked over. This has made my friendships more honest too, because people share their real worries only when they feel truly heard."
srsWords: "listen" "interrupt" "respect" "understand" "relationship"

lessonTitle: "Dealing with Conflict"
description: "Write about how you handle conflict"
icon: "🤝"
color: "#0D9488"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我和朋友也曾因为小事吵架。以前我会冷战，谁也不理谁，结果越拖越难受。现在我学着冷静下来，把感受说清楚，也听听对方的想法。大多数时候，误会解开后，我们的关系反而更好。冲突不可怕，可怕的是谁都不肯先开口。"
answer: "My friends and I have certainly had our share of arguments over small, silly matters, and in the past my usual response was to go silent and wait for the other person to apologise first. That only made the unpleasant feeling last for days, and sometimes the friendship never quite recovered. These days I try to handle disagreements in a healthier way. I give myself a little time to calm down, then I explain honestly how I feel while also making a genuine effort to hear the other side of the story. More often than not, once the misunderstanding is cleared up, our relationship becomes stronger than before, because we have proved we can survive a difficult moment together. Conflict itself is not the real danger; the true problem is when neither person is willing to be the first to speak."
core: "I used to go silent after arguments, which only prolonged the hurt. Now I calm down, explain my feelings and hear the other's view. Misunderstandings resolved this way made friendships stronger. Conflict is fine; refusing to talk is the real problem. Addressing small issues early prevents big fights."
acceptAlso: "Arguments once sent me into a sulk that stretched the pain for days. I now take time to cool off, then speak honestly and listen to the other perspective. Clearing up the misunderstanding usually leaves the bond stronger. The danger is not disagreement itself but two people too proud to speak first. We also agreed to talk about small annoyances early, before they grow into the kind of argument that damages trust."
srsWords: "conflict" "argument" "calm" "communicate" "apologize" "friendship"

I have never forgotten that kindness, and it has changed the way I treat people around me. I now try to notice when someone is struggling and lend a hand, because I hope to become the sort of person who quietly makes another person's burden lighter."
core: "When illness made me miss school, my deskmate lent me her notes and tutored me daily for two weeks without tiring. I caught up quickly and never forgot. Her kindness made me want to help others the same way. Her patient help changed far more than just my grades."
acceptAlso: "Illness once left me far behind in class and anxious, until my deskmate lent her notes and patiently explained lessons every afternoon for two weeks. I recovered my footing and passed the next test. Her generosity stays with me and inspires me to notice and ease others' struggles. I still keep the notes she lent me, and they remind me how much difference a few patient afternoons can make to a struggling student."

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我觉得好邻居不需要天天来往，但要在关键时刻靠得住。我家楼上的阿姨就是这样：谁家收快递忘了拿，她会帮忙收着；晚上太吵，她也会客气地提醒。她让我明白，好的邻里关系是互相体谅。一点点善意，就能让一栋楼变得温暖。"
answer: "In my view a good neighbour does not need to visit every day or know all your secrets; what matters is that they can be relied on when it counts. The woman who lives upstairs in my building is exactly this kind of person. If someone forgets to collect a delivery, she happily keeps it safe, and if a party gets too noisy late at night, she reminds us politely instead of complaining angrily. She never makes a fuss about small problems, yet she is always ready with a helping hand or a friendly word. From her I have learned that good neighbourly relations rest on consideration and a willingness to forgive little annoyances. A small amount of genuine goodwill is enough to turn a cold block of flats into a place that feels warm and safe, and that is something everyone can help create."
core: "A good neighbour is reliable when needed, not necessarily close every day. The woman upstairs stores forgotten deliveries and politely reminds us about noise. She shows me that consideration matters. Small kindness can make a whole building feel warm. Small everyday gestures build real trust and warmth over time."
acceptAlso: "A good neighbour need not be constantly present, just dependable in small moments. The lady upstairs keeps our missed parcels and handles noise complaints with gentle politeness. She has taught me that consideration and forgiveness make community life pleasant. A little goodwill warms an entire building. We also take in one another's parcels and water the plants when someone travels, small gestures that slowly build real trust and warmth between neighbours."
srsWords: "neighbour" "reliable" "considerate" "delivery" "polite" "community"

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我不在乎朋友有多少，而在乎他们是什么样的人。我更愿意和诚实、积极的人在一起，因为他们让我变得更好。表面上热闹的关系，如果缺少真诚，很快就会散。真正的朋友会在你需要时出现，也会在你走偏时提醒你。我很庆幸身边有这样几个人。"
answer: "I do not care how many friends I have; what matters to me is what kind of people they are. I would much rather spend time with a few honest, positive individuals than with a large crowd of shallow acquaintances, because good friends quietly shape who we become. A relationship that is lively on the surface but lacks sincerity will not last long once real difficulties appear. A true friend turns up when you need support, and is also brave enough to warn you when you are heading in the wrong direction, even if the truth is hard to hear. I consider myself very fortunate to have several such people in my life, and I try to be that sort of friend to them in return, because the company we keep is one of the most important choices we ever make."
core: "I value the quality of friends, not the number. I prefer honest, positive people who make me better. Lively but insincere bonds fade, while true friends support and warn us. I am lucky to have a few such friends and try to be one in return. Support must be given, not just received."
acceptAlso: "Friendship, for me, is about character rather than quantity. A handful of honest, upbeat companions improves me far more than a noisy crowd. Shallow ties collapse under pressure; real friends offer support and honest warnings. I am grateful for a few such people and strive to deserve their friendship. I try hard to be that kind of friend too, because honesty and support must be given as well as received, not simply expected from others."
srsWords: "friend" "quality" "honest" "positive" "sincere" "fortunate"

lessonTitle: "Keeping in Touch"
description: "Write about keeping in touch with old friends"
icon: "🤝"
color: "#0D9488"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我们常常因为忙，和老朋友慢慢失去联系。等回过神来，才发现已经好几年没说话了。我以前也是这样，后来我给自己定了个小规矩：每个月给一位老朋友发消息。哪怕只是问问近况，也让我觉得友情还在。联系不需要理由，想起来就该去做。"
answer: "It is surprisingly easy to lose touch with old friends simply because life keeps us busy, and before we notice, several years have slipped by without a single conversation. I certainly fell into this pattern myself, until I decided to make a small rule for my own life: every month I send a message to one old friend I have not spoken to for a while. The message does not need to be long or important; even a simple question about how they have been is enough to reopen the connection and remind both of us that the friendship still matters. More often than not, the other person is delighted to hear from me, and we end up chatting for an hour. Staying in touch does not require a special reason or a perfect moment."
core: "Busy lives slowly separate us from old friends. I now message one old friend each month, even just to ask how they are. It keeps the friendship alive, and they are usually delighted. No special reason is needed to reconnect. A short voice message can keep an old bond alive."
acceptAlso: "Old friendships quietly fade when we let busyness take over, as I discovered after years of silence. I began contacting one old friend monthly with a simple hello, and these small messages revived bonds on both sides. There is no need for a grand reason; thinking of someone is enough to reach out. Sometimes a short voice message is enough, and hearing an old friend's laugh can brighten an ordinary afternoon."
srsWords: "old" "friends" "contact" "monthly" "message" "reconnect" "busy"
