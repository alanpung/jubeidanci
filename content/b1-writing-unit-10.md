lessonTitle: "If I Could Fly"
description: "Write about the ability to fly"
icon: "🌟"
color: "#DC2626"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我常常幻想自己能飞。想飞的时候，只要轻轻一跳，就能升到空中，越过高楼和云层，俯瞰整个城市。没有红绿灯，没有拥挤的车厢，只有风和阳光。这个梦也许永远不会实现，但它让我想到：换个高度看世界，烦恼会变得多小啊。"
answer: "I often find myself daydreaming about the simple, wonderful ability to fly. In my imagination, whenever I felt the urge, I could give a little leap and rise gently into the air, soaring above the tallest buildings and drifting through soft white clouds while the whole city spread out far below me. Up there there would be no traffic lights, no crowded trains and no noisy classrooms, only the fresh wind and warm sunlight. I know this dream will probably never come true in reality, and I am quite all right with that, because it still teaches me something valuable. From a greater height, the problems that loom so large in my daily life would shrink to tiny specks, and perhaps the real gift of imagination is that it lets us rise above our troubles for a moment and see the world with new, lighter eyes."
core: "I often dream of flying: a small leap lifts me above buildings and clouds, with no traffic or crowds, only wind and sun. It may never happen, but it reminds me that from a higher view our problems shrink and the world looks lighter. I would fly over my old school too."
acceptAlso: "The fantasy of flight delights me: one jump and I float above the rooftops through clouds, leaving traffic and noise far beneath. Though it will never be real, it offers a useful lesson. Lifted higher, our worries become small dots, and imagination gives us fresh, gentler eyes. Sometimes I imagine flying over my old school, looking down at the playground where I spent so many happy afternoons as a child."
srsWords: "fly" "dream" "clouds" "height" "problems" "imagination" "light"

lessonTitle: "A World Without Electricity"
description: "Write about life without electricity"
icon: "🌟"
color: "#DC2626"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "如果有一天电突然消失了，生活会变成什么样？起初大家一定会手忙脚乱，因为手机、电灯、冰箱全用不了。但慢慢地，人们也许会重新学会点蜡烛、写信、面对面聊天。这样的想象让我明白：我们依赖电太久，也该想想，没有它我们还能不能好好生活。"
answer: "I sometimes wonder what life would look like if electricity were suddenly to vanish one morning. At first, of course, everyone would be thrown into confusion, because our phones would die, the lights would go out and the food in our refrigerators would begin to spoil. We depend on power so completely that we rarely stop to think about it. Yet as the days passed, people might slowly rediscover older, simpler skills: lighting candles at dusk, writing letters by hand and talking to one another face to face instead of staring at glowing screens. I find this imaginary scenario strangely useful, because it forces me to ask honest questions about my own dependence."
core: "If electricity vanished, phones, lights and fridges would stop, causing chaos. Over time we might light candles, write letters and talk face to face. This daydream makes me question my dependence and be more grateful, yet less reliant, on modern power. We might share stories at dusk again."
acceptAlso: "Imagining a sudden world without electricity is both exciting and sobering. Initial panic would give way to candlelight, handwritten notes and real conversation as forgotten skills returned. The exercise teaches me how deeply I rely on power and nudges me to be both more grateful for it and more able to live without it. We might also gather at dusk to share stories, rediscovering the simple pleasure of sitting together in the dark."
srsWords: "electricity" "vanish" "imagine" "dependence" "candle" "simple"

icon: "🌟"
color: "#DC2626"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我梦想中的房子不必很大，但要明亮又温馨。它最好在海边，早上能听到浪声。客厅有一整面墙的书架，厨房干净宽敞，院子里种着花草。我最想要一间面朝大海的书房，可以坐在窗边看书、写字。家不一定要豪华，舒服和有爱才重要。"
answer: "The house of my dreams does not need to be enormous or grand, but it must be bright, warm and full of character. Ideally it would stand near the sea, so that I could wake up each morning to the gentle sound of waves. The living room would have a whole wall of bookshelves, the kitchen would be clean and spacious enough to cook with friends, and a small garden outside would be filled with flowers and herbs. More than anything else, I would love a quiet study facing the water, where I could sit by the window for hours with a good book, writing or simply watching the light change on the surface. To me a home does not have to be luxurious or impressive to visitors. What truly matters is that it feels comfortable, peaceful and filled with the warmth of the people who live there."
core: "My dream house is modest but bright and warm, ideally near the sea with waves at dawn. It would have a wall of books, a big kitchen, a garden and a seaside study for reading and writing. Luxury matters less than comfort and love. Second-hand furniture would fill it."
acceptAlso: "I picture a dream home that is cozy rather than huge, set near the sea so mornings begin with waves. Books line the living room, the kitchen welcomes friends, flowers fill the garden and a seaside study invites long hours of reading. Comfort and love, not luxury, define this place. I would fill it with second-hand furniture and books, because a home should carry stories rather than look like a showroom."
srsWords: "dream" "house" "sea" "bookshelf" "garden" "study" "warmth"

lessonTitle: "If I Were Invisible"
description: "Write about being invisible for a day"
icon: "🌟"
color: "#DC2626"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "如果我有一天能隐身，我会怎么用这段时间？我想悄悄走进图书馆，看看人们专注读书的样子；也想溜进动物园，近距离观察动物不被打扰的举动。也许我还会跟在朋友身后，听听他们说我的好话。不过我想，隐身一天后，我更会珍惜被人看见、被人记住的感觉。"
answer: "If I were given the chance to become invisible for a single day, I am not sure I would use it for anything grand or mischievous. I think I would quietly slip into a library and watch people absorbed in their books, their faces lit by concentration and curiosity. I would love to visit a zoo as well, observing how animals behave when no one is watching or disturbing them. Perhaps I would even follow my friends around and listen to the kind things they say when I am not in the room, which is something people rarely get to experience. Yet I suspect that after a day of being unseen, I would treasure far more the ordinary feeling of being noticed and remembered by others. This little fantasy teaches me that being seen, even in small ways, is a surprisingly important part of feeling alive and connected."
core: "If invisible for a day, I would watch readers in a library, observe undisturbed animals and hear friends' kind words. But afterwards I would value being seen and remembered. The fantasy shows how important recognition is. I would stand on a busy street and quietly watch people go by."
acceptAlso: "Given a day of invisibility, I would wander through libraries and zoos, watching people and animals unaware of me, maybe even hear my friends' honest praise. Yet the experience would likely make me cherish being noticed. The daydream reveals how much recognition and connection matter to us. I would also stand on a busy street unnoticed, wondering where all those hurried people are going and what small worries they carry that day."
srsWords: "invisible" "library" "animals" "friends" "seen" "value"

color: "#DC2626"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "如果给二十年后的自己写一封信，我想问：你还记得现在的梦想吗？你有没有变得更有勇气，还是被生活磨平了棱角？我希望你依然爱读书，依然会为小事感动，依然保留一点天真。不管你成了什么样，请别忘了今天的自己，也别忘了为什么出发。"
answer: "If I could write a letter to myself twenty years from now, the first thing I would ask is whether I still remember the dreams I hold so dearly today. Have I grown braver and wiser, or has daily life slowly worn down the sharp edges of my spirit? I hope that I will still love reading, that I will still be moved by small, beautiful things, and that I will have kept a little of the innocence and wonder I feel now. I want to know whether I became the sort of person I once imagined, someone who is gentle but strong, successful but never too proud. Whatever has happened, whatever shape my life has taken, I would tell that future self not to forget the person writing this letter today, and never to forget why the journey began."
core: "In a letter to my future self I would ask: do you still remember today's dreams? Are you braver or worn down? I hope you still love reading and small joys. Whatever you become, never forget today's self or why you began. A sense of humour matters more than success."
acceptAlso: "Writing to myself twenty years ahead, I would ask if the dreams remain, if courage grew or faded, if books and small wonders still move me. My wish is to stay gentle, strong and humble. Whatever I become, I must remember this starting self and the reasons I set out. I would also ask whether I still laugh at my own mistakes, because keeping a sense of humour matters more than any success."
srsWords: "future" "self" "letter" "dreams" "courage" "remember" "begin"

description: "Write about an imaginary friend"
icon: "🌟"
color: "#DC2626"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "小时候我有一个想象中的朋友，我叫他小云。他是一只会说话的白色小鸟，会在我孤单时陪我玩，也会在我犯错时安慰我。别人看不见他，但我知道他在。长大后我虽然不再看见小云，但那种有人陪伴、有人倾听的感觉，我一直记得，也一直想给别人。"
answer: "When I was small I had an imaginary friend whom I named Little Cloud. He was a talking white bird who would keep me company when I felt lonely, playing games in the garden or simply sitting on my windowsill while I poured out my childish troubles. He never judged me, and even when I had done something wrong he would comfort me rather than scold. Other people could not see him, of course, but to me he was completely real, and the comfort he brought was genuine. As I grew older, Little Cloud gradually faded away, as such friends often do, and I stopped seeing him."
core: "As a child I had an imaginary friend, a talking white bird called Little Cloud, who comforted me and listened when I was lonely. Others could not see him, but he felt real. Though he faded as I grew, the feeling of companionship stayed, inspiring me to be that presence for others."
acceptAlso: "My childhood imaginary friend was a white talking bird named Little Cloud, a patient companion who never judged and always listened. Invisible to others but vivid to me, he eased my loneliness. He eventually faded, yet the comfort of being accompanied endured, and it drives me to offer that same patient presence to people in my life. I sometimes wonder whether every lonely child invents such a friend, and whether the habit says something about our need to be heard."
srsWords: "imaginary" "friend" "childhood" "bird" "company" "listen" "comfort"

sentence: "我理想中的完美一天，是从睡到自然醒开始的。早上吃一顿丰盛的早餐，然后去海边散步，听浪声、吹海风。下午找一家安静的咖啡馆看书，什么也不赶。傍晚和朋友吃顿简单的晚饭，说说笑笑。晚上早点睡，带着满足的心情结束这一天。这样普通又珍贵的日子，就是我的幸福。"
answer: "My idea of a perfect day begins with waking up naturally, without the harsh ringing of an alarm dragging me from sleep. I would enjoy a slow, generous breakfast and then take a long walk by the sea, listening to the waves and letting the salty wind clear my head. In the afternoon I would find a quiet little cafe and sit there with a good book for hours, with no deadlines and no obligations pulling me away. As evening came, I would share a simple meal with close friends, laughing and talking about nothing in particular, and then head home early to sleep with a contented heart. There would be nothing spectacular about such a day, no grand adventure or great achievement, and that is exactly why it would be perfect for me. Happiness, I have realised, often lives in these gentle, unhurried moments."
core: "My perfect day starts with waking naturally, then a big breakfast and a seaside walk with waves and wind. The afternoon is for reading in a quiet cafe, and the evening for a simple dinner with friends and early sleep. Ordinary and unhurried, it is exactly what makes it perfect."
acceptAlso: "A flawless day, to me, begins with natural sleep and a leisurely breakfast, followed by a seaside walk in the salty wind. I would read undisturbed in a small cafe all afternoon, share a simple laughing dinner with friends and sleep early, content. Nothing grand, just gentle, unrushed moments, which is precisely why it would be perfect. I would leave my phone in another room, so nothing could interrupt the slow, gentle rhythm of those hours."
srsWords: "perfect" "day" "wake" "seaside" "cafe" "friends" "content"

answer: "I wonder what the world would be like if animals could suddenly speak to us, and the questions I would ask are surprisingly simple. First I would ask my cat what exactly she watches so intently from the windowsill for hours each day. I would love to know whether dogs truly understand their owners' moods, or whether the loyalty we admire is something deeper we have never properly grasped. Perhaps animals would tell us that they have long understood things we human beings overlook, such as the value of silence, the comfort of simple companionship and the foolishness of constant hurry. I imagine their advice would be quiet and wise, delivered without judgement."
core: "If animals could talk, I would ask my cat what she watches at the window and whether dogs understand our moods. They might teach us about silence, companionship and slowing down. This daydream makes me respect and protect animals more. They might complain about how careless we are."
acceptAlso: "Were animals able to speak, I would ask my cat about her endless window-gazing and whether dogs sense our feelings. They might reveal wisdom about silence, company and our pointless rush. The fantasy, playful as it is, deepens my respect for them and reminds me that other lives share our world and deserve care. I suspect their largest complaint would be about how careless we are with the places they call home."

---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "想象一下住在月球上：早上醒来，看到巨大的地球悬在灰色的天空中；走起路来轻飘飘的，一跳就能跳很高；晚上没有云，星星亮得惊人。当然也有麻烦：没有空气和水，得住在密封的屋里，还要穿厚厚的宇航服。这样的生活既奇妙又孤独，让我更加珍惜地球这个家。"
answer: "Just imagine living on the moon. Each morning you would wake to see the enormous Earth hanging in a grey, airless sky, and because gravity is so weak, even a small jump would send you floating high above the dusty ground. At night there would be no clouds to blur the view, and the stars would shine with a brilliant, sharp clarity impossible to see from home. Of course, such a life would have serious difficulties too. With no air to breathe and no liquid water, we would have to live inside sealed stations and wear thick suits whenever we stepped outside. The silence and emptiness would feel strange, perhaps lonely. Thinking about this wild possibility makes me see our own planet in a new light."
core: "Living on the moon means seeing Earth in the sky, floating in low gravity and brilliant starry nights. But there is no air or water, so we would need sealed homes and thick suits. The idea is exciting yet lonely, and it makes me cherish Earth more. A small garden would remind me of home."
acceptAlso: "Moon life would be extraordinary: Earth looming in a grey sky, effortless high jumps and dazzling starlight without clouds. Yet without air or water we would be confined to sealed stations and heavy suits, and the silence might feel lonely. The fantasy makes me love our crowded, living Earth all the more. I would bring a small garden too, because green plants might be the best reminder of the world we left below."
srsWords: "moon" "Earth" "gravity" "stars" "air" "Earth" "home"

lessonTitle: "The Power of Imagination"
description: "Write about why imagination matters"
icon: "🌟"
color: "#DC2626"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我觉得想象力是人类最宝贵的能力之一。它让我们在无聊时创造故事，在困难时看到希望，在平凡的日子里发现奇迹。科学家靠想象提出新想法，作家靠想象创造世界。对孩子来说，一把扫帚可以是魔法扫帚，一个纸盒可以是城堡。失去了想象，生活就会变得灰暗。我希望自己永远保留这份能力。"
answer: "I believe imagination is one of the most precious abilities human beings possess, because it lets us create stories when we are bored, find hope when life is difficult and discover wonder in completely ordinary days. Scientists rely on imagination to form new ideas before they can prove them, and writers use it to build entire worlds that feel real to millions of readers. For a child, a broom can become a magic flying stick and a cardboard box can be a castle with secret doors, and that is not silliness but a powerful way of understanding the world. As we grow older, many of us unfortunately lose this gift, and life becomes greyer and more rigid as a result."
core: "Imagination is precious: it creates stories, offers hope in hard times and finds wonder in ordinary life. Scientists and writers need it, and children turn boxes into castles. Many adults lose it and life turns grey. I hope to keep mine forever. Reading and daydreaming help protect it."
acceptAlso: "Imagination may be our greatest gift, spinning tales in boredom, hope in hardship and magic in routine. It drives scientific ideas and fictional worlds, and lets children see castles in cardboard boxes. Too many adults let it fade and life narrows. I want to protect this source of creativity and joy always. I try to protect it by reading widely and daydreaming without guilt, because a world without imagination would be a much poorer place."
srsWords: "imagination" "precious" "hope" "wonder" "creativity" "child"
