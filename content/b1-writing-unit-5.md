lessonTitle: "A Useful App"
description: "Write about a useful app"
icon: "🤖"
color: "#DB2777"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "我几乎每天都用一款翻译软件。遇到不认识的单词，我几秒钟就能查到它在完整句子里的用法。它还会帮我保存生词，方便晚上复习。我最喜欢的是它能朗读难读的单词，让我说英语更有信心。有了它，我学英语轻松多了。"
answer: "One app I use almost every day is a translation tool on my phone, and it has made a real difference to the way I study English. When I read an article and meet a word I do not know, I can look it up in seconds and see how it is used inside a full sentence, not just a bare definition. The app also keeps a personal word list for me, so I can review new vocabulary later in the evening while the words are still fresh in my mind. What I like most is that it can pronounce difficult words out loud, which helps me speak with far more confidence than before. Thanks to this small piece of software, learning a foreign language feels less like hard work and more like a natural part of my daily routine, and I have improved steadily since I started using it."
core: "I use a translation app every day to study English. It lets me look up new words in seconds and shows how they appear in real sentences. It saves my new vocabulary so I can review it later. Best of all, it pronounces hard words aloud, which boosts my speaking confidence. It has made learning much easier for me."
acceptAlso: "A translation program on my phone has become an essential study partner. Whenever I meet unfamiliar vocabulary, I check it quickly and learn how native speakers actually use it in context. The app stores my new words and reminds me to revise them, and its voice feature teaches me correct pronunciation. Because of it, studying feels lighter and more enjoyable, and my English has grown stronger step by step. I also review my saved words before bed, so the habit quietly fits into every part of my day."
srsWords: "translation" "app" "vocabulary" "pronounce" "review" "study"

sentence: "我十岁生日时有了第一台电脑，又旧又慢，打开一个网页要等很久。可我就是被它迷住了。我用它写作业、画画，还第一次学会了打字。后来电脑换了又好几次，但第一台那种新奇和兴奋的感觉，我一直记得。它打开了通向新世界的大门。"
answer: "I got my first computer for my tenth birthday, and although it was old and painfully slow, I was completely fascinated by it from the moment it started up. Opening a single web page could take what felt like ages, and sometimes it froze without warning, yet I did not mind at all. I used it to write my homework, draw simple pictures, and, for the first time in my life, learn how to type properly with all ten fingers. Over the years I have owned several better and faster machines, but I still remember the strange excitement of that first one as if it were yesterday. In a very real sense, that clumsy old computer opened a door for me and showed me how much there was to discover beyond the small world I had known until then."
core: "I got my first computer at ten. It was old and slow, but I loved it. I wrote homework, drew pictures and learned to type on it. Later I had better computers, yet I still remember that first excitement. It opened a door to a whole new world for me."
acceptAlso: "My first computer arrived on my tenth birthday, a slow and ancient machine that often froze. I was entranced anyway, using it for schoolwork, drawing and my first typing lessons. Modern devices are far quicker, but nothing matches the thrill of that first one. It revealed to me a bigger world and started my lifelong curiosity about technology. I remember saving pocket money for a small printer, and printing my first drawing felt like real magic."
srsWords: "computer" "birthday" "slow" "type" "doorway" "curious"

[writing]

answer: "I check social media for a little while every day, and it plays a noticeable part in my daily life. At the same time, I have noticed that it is very easy to keep scrolling without noticing the time, and more than once I have looked up to find that half an hour has quietly disappeared. To deal with this, I now set a timer before I open the apps, and when it rings I put the phone down at once. In my view these platforms are useful tools that connect us, but they should never become the main character of my life, and I try to remember that every single day."
core: "I use social media daily to see friends news and learn things. But I sometimes scroll for ages and waste half an hour. Now I set a timer and stop when it rings. It is a useful tool, but it should not be the centre of my life."
acceptAlso: "Social media is part of my everyday routine, keeping me close to friends and bringing me news and ideas. Yet I often lose track of time while scrolling and realize too late that minutes have slipped away. I have started using a timer to control my habits. These apps should serve me, not rule me, and I work hard to keep that balance. I follow a few science accounts now, so my feed leaves me knowing something new instead of merely older."
srsWords: "social" "media" "scroll" "timer" "friends" "balance"

answer: "About six months ago I decided to learn how to write computer code, and the beginning was much harder than I had expected. A screen full of strange letters and symbols made my head spin, and even the simplest little program seemed to go wrong for reasons I could not understand. Still, I kept practising a little every single day, and whenever an error appeared I learned to read it carefully and trace the problem step by step rather than giving up. When I finally got a tiny game of my own to run smoothly for the first time, the feeling of pride and joy was almost impossible to describe. Coding has taught me far more than technical skills; above all it has given me patience and a practical way of solving problems that I can use in every other area of my life."
core: "I started learning to code six months ago. At first the symbols confused me and my programs always failed. But I practised daily and fixed errors patiently. When my first small game worked, I felt incredibly proud. Coding taught me patience and how to solve problems. I keep notes of common mistakes."
acceptAlso: "My journey into programming began half a year ago with confusion and constant errors. The strange syntax overwhelmed me, yet I practised every day and learned to work through each bug calmly. Running my first simple game was a proud moment I will not forget. Beyond the technical know-how, coding has given me patience and a clear method for tackling difficulties wherever they appear. I keep a notebook of common errors, which makes each new session faster and far less frustrating than before."
srsWords: "code" "programming" "error" "practise" "problem" "patient"

---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English"
sentence: "上周我的电脑突然连不上网，而第二天我就要交作业。我起初很慌，后来冷静下来，先重启，又检查设置，最后发现是一个小插头松了。十分钟后网络恢复了。这次经历让我明白，遇到技术问题别慌张，一步一步排查，往往能自己解决。"
answer: "Last week my laptop suddenly stopped connecting to the internet, and I had an important assignment due the very next morning, so I felt a wave of panic at first. After a few minutes, though, I forced myself to stay calm and think clearly. I restarted the machine, checked the network settings one by one, and finally discovered that a small cable at the back had come loose. Within ten minutes the connection was working again and I finished my homework on time. That experience taught me a lesson I will not forget: when technology misbehaves, panic never helps, but patient, step-by-step investigation usually reveals a surprisingly simple cause. Since then I have become much more confident about fixing small problems on my own instead of immediately asking someone else for help."
core: "Last week my computer lost its internet connection before a deadline. I panicked, then calmed down, restarted it and checked the settings. A loose cable was the cause, and I fixed it in ten minutes. I learned to stay calm and solve tech problems step by step. A short checklist guides me now."
acceptAlso: "My laptop went offline the night before an assignment was due, which made me anxious. I took a breath, restarted it and examined the settings carefully, eventually finding a loose cable at the back. Ten minutes later I was online. The episode showed me that calm, methodical checking solves most small technical troubles, and I now trust my own ability to handle them. I now check cables and power first, a simple habit that has saved me from panic more than once."
srsWords: "laptop" "internet" "cable" "calm" "problem" "solve"

core: "Smartphones are part of my daily routine: alarm, messages, information, payments and music. But they also create dependence. Families sit together yet stare at their own screens. I believe knowing when to put the phone away is more important than having the latest model. I keep it away during family meals."
acceptAlso: "My phone fills many roles each day, waking me up, keeping me in touch, finding facts and handling payments. However, this convenience has a cost, as we grow reliant on glowing screens even during family time when conversation should fill the room. I think the real skill today is putting the device aside at the right moment and truly being with the people nearby. I leave my phone in another room during dinner, and our conversations have grown longer and warmer since."
