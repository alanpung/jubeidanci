lessonTitle: "My Morning Routine"
description: "Write about your morning routine"
icon: "🏠"
color: "#CA8A04"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this passage in English in three short paragraphs: an opening, a middle and an ending"
sentence: "我每天早上六点半起床。先喝一杯温水，再拉窗帘让阳光进来。然后我会花十分钟整理床铺、做简单的伸展，最后吃早餐、出门。这个顺序看似普通，却让我一天有个好开始。有了固定的早晨，我不再慌慌张张地赶时间。（请写成三段：开头点题、中间展开、结尾收束。字数100-150）"
answer: "My morning routine begins at half past six, when the alarm rings and I force myself out of the warm blankets. || The first thing I do is drink a glass of warm water, which somehow wakes up my whole body, and then I open the curtains to let the morning light fill the room. After that I spend about ten minutes making my bed and doing a few gentle stretches to loosen the stiffness of sleep. Only then do I sit down for breakfast and prepare to leave the house. || Though these actions may seem ordinary and unremarkable, following the same order every day gives my whole day a calm and steady beginning."
core: "I get up at 6:30, drink warm water, open the curtains, make my bed and stretch before breakfast. This simple routine gives me a calm start and stops the morning rush, leaving me organised and ready for the day. I keep a similar simple order even on busy weekends."
acceptAlso: "Each morning I rise at half six, drink warm water, open the curtains to the light, tidy my bed and stretch briefly before eating. || Repeating this order creates a peaceful start and frees me from frantic rushing. That quiet sense of order carries through the rest of my day. || Weekends are more relaxed, but even then I try to keep the same basic order because it helps me feel settled for the day."
srsWords: "morning" "routine" "wake" "curtain" "stretch" "breakfast" "calm"

On ordinary days I always complain that time races past far too quickly, but on that slow, grey afternoon it seemed to stretch out gently around me. There was nowhere to be and nothing urgent to finish, and strangely, doing almost nothing left me feeling more rested and renewed than a busy holiday often does. || I have come to treasure such quiet rainy days, because they give me permission to stop, to breathe and to remember that rest is not laziness but a necessary part of a balanced life."
core: "Last Saturday it rained all day, so I stayed home with hot tea and a book, listening to rain on the window. Time slowed down, and doing nothing recharged me. I treasure such quiet rainy days as real rest. Writing letters suits the slow rhythm of a rainy afternoon."
acceptAlso: "A full day of rain last Saturday kept me indoors with tea, a long-awaited book and the gentle drumming on the glass. || Unlike my usual rushed days, time seemed to relax, and idleness restored my energy. I now value these grey, silent afternoons as essential rest rather than wasted time. || I sometimes write letters on such afternoons, and the slow rhythm suits a day when nothing outside is asking for attention."

acceptAlso: "Weekly market trips are a pleasure I look forward to, selecting fresh produce and chatting with familiar vendors. || Supermarkets offer convenience, but the market offers human warmth and local flavours. I bring a list to prevent overspending, and the tired walk home with full bags fills me with a grounded sense of caring for myself. || I have got to know the sellers well, and they often set aside the best produce for me, which feels personal."
