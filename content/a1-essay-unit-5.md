lessonTitle: "Lesson 10: Traffic Lights"
description: "Write an English essay about traffic lights"
icon: "🚌"
color: "#06B6D4"
---

[writing]
text: "Write this essay in English"
sentence: "路上有红绿灯。红灯停，绿灯走，黄灯等一等。这是交通规则。开车的和走路的人都要遵守。红灯时不能过马路。绿灯时可以走。要看左右再过马路。交通灯保护我们的安全。大家都要守规则。"
answer: "There are traffic lights on the road. Stop at red, go at green, and wait at yellow. These are traffic rules. Drivers and walkers must follow them. At a red light you cannot cross the road. At a green light you can walk. Look left and right before you cross. Traffic lights keep us safe. Everyone must follow the rules."
acceptAlso: "Roads have traffic lights. Red means stop, green means go, yellow means wait. These are traffic rules. Drivers and walkers must obey. You cannot cross on red. You can walk on green. Look both ways before crossing. Lights keep us safe. Everyone must follow rules."
srsWords: "traffic" "light" "red" "green" "safe"
