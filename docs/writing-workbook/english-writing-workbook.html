English Writing Exercise Workbook

CEFR Levels: A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · Advanced — Progressive scaffolding for confident English writing

How to Use This Workbook

Each level follows a progressive scaffolding approach — as your English improves, the Chinese support is gradually removed and replaced with English vocabulary hints, pushing you to write independently and closer to model-answer quality.

B1 · Exercise 1: The Importance of Reading

Requirements: (3–4 paragraphs, 100–150 words)
Prompt: Write an essay about why reading is important. Discuss the benefits of reading and how it can improve our lives.
中文结构提示 (Simple Chinese — Core Structure)

第一段（引言）：阅读对我们的生活很重要。它不仅帮助我们学习知识，还能带来快乐。

第二段（好处一）：阅读可以开阔我们的眼界。通过书本，我们可以了解不同的文化和地方，不用出门也能看到世界。

第三段（好处二）：阅读还可以提高我们的语言能力。读得越多，词汇量越大，写作也会更好。

第四段（结论）：总之，阅读是一种好习惯。我们应该每天花时间读书，让阅读成为生活的一部分。

In recent years, social media has become an integral part of young people's daily lives. While it offers numerous benefits, it also brings significant challenges that cannot be ignored.

Model Answer: Advanced · Exercise 1 (The Ethics of Artificial Intelligence)

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence has sparked a global debate concerning its ethical implications. While AI undoubtedly offers unprecedented opportunities to improve human life, it also raises profound ethical questions that demand careful consideration.

One of the most significant benefits of AI lies in its potential to revolutionize healthcare, scientific research, and education. AI-powered diagnostic tools can detect diseases earlier and more accurately than traditional methods. In education, personalized learning platforms can adapt to individual students' needs, making quality education more accessible. These applications demonstrate AI's capacity to serve the greater good.

However, the risks are equally substantial. Algorithmic bias is a pressing concern: AI systems trained on biased data can perpetuate and even amplify existing social inequalities, leading to discriminatory outcomes in hiring, lending, and criminal justice. Furthermore, the mass collection of data required to train AI models raises serious privacy concerns. The potential for surveillance and the erosion of personal privacy represent a dystopian scenario that society must actively prevent.

The economic impact of AI also cannot be overlooked. While automation creates new industries, it also threatens to displace millions of workers, potentially widening socioeconomic inequality. Without adequate reskilling programs and social safety nets, entire communities could be left behind.

To navigate these challenges, it is imperative that society establishes a robust regulatory framework. This includes mandating transparency and accountability in AI systems, ensuring human oversight in autonomous decision-making, and developing ethical guidelines that prioritize human dignity. Governments, technologists, and civil society must engage in collaborative governance to ensure that AI development serves the public interest.

Ultimately, the trajectory of AI development will be shaped by the ethical framework we establish today. By weighing the benefits against the risks and implementing responsible innovation, we can harness the potential of AI while mitigating its unintended consequences. The onus lies not only on policymakers but also on technologists and society at large to ensure that AI serves humanity rather than the other way around.

(7 paragraphs, 316 words)
